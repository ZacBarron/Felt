Built for live play

Live games are not solver games.

Opens are bigger. Pots go multiway. Stacks run deep. Aced teaches the solver baseline first. Then it teaches the adjustments that win at a real table. Every adjustment is labelled, so you know what is math and what is a read.

FAQ

Questions players ask.

Is Aced gambling?

No. Aced is a training app. There is no real money and nothing to win. You play practice hands against bots to learn better decisions.

Do I need a solver?

No. Aced uses solver style charts and explains them in plain words. You learn the decisions without running any software.

Is it for live or online poker?

Both. Charts and lessons cover live and online cash games. Live players can set their own open size, and lessons cover multiway pots and deep stacks.

I am a beginner. Can I keep up?

Yes. The basics track starts from the rules of the game. Experienced players can take the placement quiz and skip ahead.

How does the rating work?

It moves only on graded answers to questions with a right answer. It works like a chess rating and shows how sure it is while it settles.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel in Settings or your app store account. You keep the free plan and your progress.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it is open, lessons, review and drills work without a connection.
