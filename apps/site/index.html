How you get better

Play a hand. Learn the fix. Make it stick.

  1. Play

    A practice table that grades you.

    Bots play like the regs and recs at a typical card room. They limp and call too much the way real players do. When Aced can't grade a decision honestly, it says so.

  2. Learn

    Lessons that take a few minutes.

    Each screen teaches one idea with a picture. Every answer explains why. A short placement quiz lets you skip ahead.

  3. Ranges

    Charts that stick.

    Learn a chart for every seat. Quiz yourself or paint it from memory. Review brings back the hands you miss right before you forget them.

  4. Rating

    A rating you earn.

    Your Aced Rating moves only on questions with a right answer. Chips won never count, because one session is mostly luck. There are no points for showing up.

  5. Daily

    One daily challenge. Same for everyone.

    You get 5 new decisions each day. See how you compare with every player. Share your result without spoiling a hand.

Built for live play

Live games are not solver games.

Opens are bigger. Someone always limps. Someone always straddles. Aced teaches the GTO baseline first. Then it shows you how to adjust. Each tip says if it's math or a read.

FAQ

Questions players ask.

What's free?

The full basics track and the first topic of every track are free. So are daily review and drills. The daily challenge and your Aced Rating are free too.

Will it help my live 1/2 game?

Yes. Aced was built for live cash first. You can set the open size you really use. Lessons cover limpers, straddles, multiway pots and deep stacks.

I'm new to poker. Can I keep up?

Yes. The basics track starts with the rules. If you've played for years, take the placement quiz and skip ahead.

Is this GTO or exploitative?

Both. You learn the GTO baseline first. Then you learn when to leave it. Every answer says if it's math or a read.

Are the charts real solver output?

The preflop charts are close approximations of published solves. The app labels them that way. Turn and river grades come from Aced's own solver. Each solve shows how close it got.

How does the Aced Rating work?

It moves only on questions with a right answer. It works like a chess rating. While it's still settling, it tells you so.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play today's challenge and start lessons right away. Your progress saves on this device. Make an account when you want it on more than one device.

Can I import my hands?

Online hands, yes. Aced reads the text format most sites export. For live play, log your sessions and note hands on your break.

What happens after the free week?

You pay for the year unless you cancel first. Cancel in Settings or in your app store account. You keep the free plan and your progress.

What's a founding member?

A founding member is one of our first 500 players. You pay once and keep Pro for as long as Aced runs. In return you tell us what works and what doesn't.

Can I use it during a hand?

No. Most card rooms ban phones in a hand. Use Aced on a break or before you sit down.

When do the iPhone and Android apps arrive?

Soon. You can use Aced in your browser today. Leave your email and we'll send one note on launch day.

Is Aced gambling?

No. Aced is a training app. There's no real money and nothing to win. You play practice hands against bots.
